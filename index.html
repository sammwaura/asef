Transforming lives and livelihoods across Africa

ASEF aims to empower and transform lives through capacity building and technology transfer, providing pathways out of poverty and spurring economic growth.

Our work aims to equip citizens with the tools and knowledge to combat climate change, and to inspire the next generation of climate leaders.

Our vision

Championing Social Impact Investment to improve the livelihood of rural communities in Africa.

Our mission

To alleviate poverty, improve food security, provide good governance and contribute to environmental sustainability.

Our five pillars

Each pillar is a route out of poverty and toward self-reliance. Select one to read more.

We promote tree planting for carbon credit and income generation, energy conservation through new and modern technologies, and sustainability in businesses and organisations.

We encourage a whole mindset of responsibility in support of sustainability. The aim is for the beneficiaries of your Corporate Social Responsibility to reach places of dignity and self-reliance, and ideally to be able to offer CSR to others in turn.

We promote and upgrade learning materials so they suit the needs of the current generation, with a focus on entrepreneurship skills.

We build the capacity of teachers, farmers and business communities in finance, HR, tax and strategic planning, including school boards, and we support less fortunate people to pursue education.

We promote gender equality and empowerment for economic growth.

In emerging economies where job opportunities are limited, the ability of women and youth to earn an income often depends on their ability to start a business. If policy makers prioritise investment in youth and women's economic power, the global economy could grow by an additional 7% of GDP, or US$10 trillion, by 2030.

We promote agri-business that uses modern technologies, applying a whole value chain approach from farm to market.

We promote the use of technology in farming and in business enterprises, so more people can take part in the financial system and grow their income.

US$10 trillionadded to the global economy by 2030

That is the potential gain, about 7% of GDP, if policy makers prioritise investment in the economic power of youth and women. It is why gender empowerment is one of our five pillars.

How we work with partners

We help our partners move from knowing their goal to knowing how to reach it.

We combine global expertise in health, education and technology transfer with analytic rigour, practical support for decision-making and implementation, and access to peer problem-solving networks.

Together with our partners, we build self-sustaining systems that serve everyone and deliver lasting results. Then we share what we learn so others can achieve results for development too.

We collaborate with change agents around the world, in both the public and private sectors, to create strong systems that support communities toward healthy, educated lives and an informed future generation.

Our partners include

  • Government officials
  • Industry leaders
  • Civil society leaders
  • Community leaders
  • Social innovators

What we set out to do

  • Promote tree planting for carbon credit and income generation, energy conservation using modern technologies, and environmental sustainability.
  • Offer entrepreneurship training programmes at the grassroots level.
  • Promote agri-business using modern technologies through a whole value chain approach.
  • Offer training programmes to alleviate poverty at the grassroots level.
  • Promote good corporate governance, environmental sustainability and good organisation culture in the public and private sectors.

A culture built on trust and respect

Diversity, equity and inclusion are at the heart of our work environment and help advance our mission. Diversity of ideas, identities, perspectives and backgrounds is vital to who we are and what we do.

We seek people who embrace these values and will help reinforce them. Teams across the communities we serve frequently collaborate and support each other in building a better future for all of us.
